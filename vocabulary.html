How review works

Reveal a word’s meaning, then choose Again, Good, or Easy. Again schedules a review in 10 minutes. Good starts at 1 day and doubles the interval; Easy starts at 3 days and triples it. Intervals stop at one year. This simple interval schedule is optional and does not assess fluency.
