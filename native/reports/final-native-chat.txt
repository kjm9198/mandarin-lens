beautiful: **Sentence:** "The sunset over the ocean is absolutely **beautiful**."

**Explanation:** This sentence describes a scene where the sky and sea meet at sunset, creating a picture that is very pleasing to the eye. The word "beautiful" here means "very good" or "pleasant," not "pretty."
学习: **Sentence:** "I need to **study** for my math exam tomorrow."

**Explanation:** In this sentence, "study" means to spend time learning or practicing to improve your knowledge. It is the opposite of "relax" or "sleep."
